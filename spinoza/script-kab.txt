Spinoza

1. I lukan Illu neɣ Ṛebbi ur yelli d yiwen, maca d ayen yellan ? Tagi d tikti n Spinoza, yiwen ufiluẓuf yeddren di lqern wis 17, i d-yessekles deg udlis-is muccaɛen aṭas, L'Ethique.
2. ɣur-s, tella kan yiwet n tidet, d tin ur nettfakka. Yessawal-as Rebbi neɣ Agama. D ssin yesmawen i nezmer ad nefkk kan i yiwet n tɣawsa.
3. Rebbi illu ur yelli d agellid yeqqimen ɣef usgeld (trône) akkin deg igenwan, win yettḥasaben tarwa-s neɣ i tt-yeselquyen. Illu d tazmert i s-iteddu umeɣrad (l'univers), s lekmal-is.
4. Ihi nekni, Ṭjuṛ, itran, igenwan akked tal taɣawsa deg umaḍal d tugniwin kan yebnan tazmert-agi, am lemwaji yebnan illel.
5. Tikti tis snat, deg ugama ur yelli d acu i d-yettasen akka kan. Tal taɣawsa tesɛa sebba-s, akken sebba-nni diɣen ɣur-s sebba-ines, alamma d imifeḍ (jusqu'à l'infini).
6. Ihi ayen i neǧɛell d akken nekni d ilelliyen ? Nessen lebɣiwat-nneɣ akk d acu nebɣa maca ur neẓri ansi i d-kkan neɣ tisebbiwin i ten-id-yefkan.
7. Am ublaḍ ma tɛellaḍ-t deg igenni, lemmer yettxemmim, ad yenwu d akken yettferfir acku d netta i yebɣan.
8. Syen ɣer-s, bnadem ur yelli d amenkud (un empire) s daxel n wayeḍ n umenkud. Bnadem s timmat-is yettekki deg ugama am wid akk yettikkin deg-s, akken diɣen teṭṭafaṛ isuḍaf-ines.
9. Ihi Yal iman yewwid fell-as akken ad yesdum deg tlalit-is. Mara tettnerni tezmert-nneɣ, nfeṛṛeḥ, akken nettnuɣnu mara tessilwi.
10. Ihi d acu d tilelli ? Tilelli mačči d tarewla ɣef tsebbiwin, winna d awezɣi. Tilelli tettbin-d di tigzi n tsebbiwin i d-yefkan ayen s nettḥussu.
11. Win uɣen irumsa (les passions) nebla ma yegza-ten, winna d akli, ma d win yegzan ayen s yettḥussu acimi i ten-ittḥussu, winna yettuɣal d netta i d imdebbar n tudert-is.
12. Ihi, dmma ngezzu agama, dmma nettḥussu s lferḥ lqayen akked win yettdumun. Spinoza yessawal-as Tanaflit neɣ la béatitude.
13. Mačči d ayen yeɛnan tisefka n wakkin i tmettant, maca d tanaflit n dagi akked tura.
14. Ihi, asmi ara k-yawi reffu neɣ ad k-tawi tneɣnit, ma ttesteqsiḍ iman-ik s westeqsi-yagi n Spinoza.
15. Ma d qebleɣ ayen i s ttḥissuɣ akka tura, neɣ ad nadiɣ di tazwara d acu i d tisebbiwin-ines ?
