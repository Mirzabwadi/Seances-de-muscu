Diogène – D yiwen umdan ur neḥwaǧ acemmek akken ad yidir.

1. Yella ur yeksib ula d taɣawsa, Maca yeḥseb  iman-is d ilelli seg wid ur yessaweḍ yiwen deg umaḍal
2. Diogène  d Afiluzuf Agriki yeddren deg lqern wis Kuz send talalit n Ɛisa (4eme Siècle AV. JC) Yettwassen atas seg akken ur d-yecliɛ ara di temtawiyin (les conventions) yakk  d wayen icudden ɣer rrbeḥ.
3. Ɣur-s lferḥ ur yesɛa ara tuqna akked tedrimt, neɣ d tnezmart (pouvoir) neɣ ar wayen ara d-inin medden fell-ak.
4. Ibeggen ad akken tudert di tnaflit (la vie dans le bonheur)  d aseɣres n slasel icudden amdan ɣer lebɣi ur nesɛi ara azal akked tudert swayen ilaqen kan.
5. Yiwen wass, yella ibedd taqbuct deg ifasen-is, imi iwala yiwen uqcic yeswa aman seg dkakumt n ufus-is, yekerr yebra yas i teqbuct nni yeṭṭef yuɣal yenn-yas:
6. « Aqcic-agi yesseɣer-yi d akken ziɣ  mazal kesbeɣ tiɣawsiwin  ur nesɛi ara azal. »
7. Yiwen n wass nniḍen, ileḥḥu deg uvilaj n Athena, deg uzal qayli, deg yifasn-is yewwi lamba yerna tecɛel
8. Wid i t-isteqsan d acu ɛni i yettnadi, yerra-asen-d : « Ttnadiɣ ɣef urgaz. »
9. Ula d Alexandre le Grand, agellid ameqqran deg tallit-nni , ur yezmir ara ad t-isexleɛ. Asmi t-yemlal yenna-as nekk d win ara k-yefkkeɣ akk ayen tebɣiḍ neɣ tettmenniḍ, Diogène yerra-as-d : « wexxeṛ zdati-i, ad waliɣ tafat n yiṭij. »
10. IHI, Argaz ur nccud ara tudert is ɣer lebɣi, ad yuɣal yettuqadar ɣer yemdanen meṛṛa, ula ɣer igelliden d yimeṛkantiyen.
